Agentis

Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Agentis is a college project: a workspace where AI agents help a company find leads, write and send outreach, write content, build reports and run tasks on a schedule. This page explains what Agentis stores, why, and what you can do about it.

What Agentis stores

  • Your account: name and email address, used to sign you in.
  • Your company profile: the details you enter during setup, which the agents use as context.
  • Your chats: the messages you send, the agents' results, and drafts you edit or approve.
  • Files you upload for reports, and the scheduled tasks you create.
  • Connections you make to other apps (Google, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM, LinkedIn, X, Notion, Medium): the access tokens those apps give Agentis, encrypted at rest, and the name of the connected account.

How it is used

  • Only to do what you ask Agentis to do.
  • Agents act in a connected app only after you confirm: sending an email, booking a meeting, posting to LinkedIn, saving a page to Notion or a draft to Medium, or changing a CRM record. Scheduled tasks only prepare results and drafts; they don't send or post anything on their own.
  • Agentis doesn't sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Services that process your data

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Render: runs the Agentis backend.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.
  • An AI model provider: your messages, and the data an agent needs for a task, are sent to the model to write the answer.
  • Exa: web search for lead research.
  • The apps you connect, when an agent works in them for you.

Data from Google

Agentis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail and Calendar data is used only to provide the features you use (drafting replies, checking for replies, booking meetings, reports on your outreach), is not used for ads, and is not shared with anyone except the services above as needed to provide those features.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any app on the Connect page; Agentis deletes its tokens.
  • You can also remove Agentis's access from the app itself (for example in your Google or LinkedIn settings).
  • To delete your account and everything stored with it, ask the Agentis project team.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
